Pill that states a status in words with an icon; use for room readiness, unavailability reasons and reservation stage.

```jsx
<StatusBadge tone="ok">Posprzątany, gotowy</StatusBadge>
<StatusBadge tone="warn" icon="clock-3">Gotowy od ok. 14:00</StatusBadge>
<StatusBadge tone="busy" icon="lock">Zajęty do 02.01.2025</StatusBadge>
<StatusBadge tone="off" icon="wrench">Wyłączony do 30.12.2024</StatusBadge>
```
